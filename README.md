#  Motorsport Decarbonization & Economic Abatement Analysis
### Benchmarking Formula 1 Scope 3 Freight Logistics against Formula E Sustainability Standards


##  Executive Summary
This project evaluates the economic efficiency of decarbonization strategies across motorsport freight operations. Utilizing standard **GHG Protocol Scope 3 accounting** and **Marginal Abatement Cost (MAC)** modeling, this study benchmarks traditional sequential race scheduling against regionalized logistics frameworks (pioneered by Formula E).

---

##  Key Findings & Environmental Economics Metrics

* **Scope 3 Baseline Emissions:** Traditional long-haul sequential race legs generate **~3,627 tCO2e** in freight logistics across three representative circuits.
* **Regional Calendar Optimization:** Transitioning to pre-staged sea freight and regional road freight reduces baseline emissions by **>97%** for those legs.
* **Marginal Abatement Cost Efficiency:**
  * **Regional Calendar Clustering:** **$9.19 / tCO2e** saved *(Most cost-effective structural intervention)*.
  * **HVO Biofuel Road Transition:** **$13.44 / tCO2e** saved *(Low-cost operational switch)*.
  * **Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF):** **$180.00 / tCO2e** saved *(High capital intensity abatement option)*.

---

##  Methodology & Frameworks Applied
1. **GHG Protocol Scope 3 Category 4:** Upstream Transportation & Distribution.
2. **DEFRA Carbon Intensity Factors:** Long-haul air freight (602 g CO2e/t-km), Container Sea Freight (16 g CO2e/t-km), Biofuel Trucking (19 g CO2e/t-km).
3. **Marginal Abatement Cost Formula:** **Abatement Cost ($/tCO2e) = Extra Cost ($) / CO2 Emissions Reduced (tCO2e)**
